# Compression Dividend Benchmark — Study 006

## Canonical Lexical Fidelity Without Protected Target Disclosure

Study ID: CD-WORKLOAD-20260830-006

Module ID: CD006-CANONICAL-LEXICAL-ENRICHED-BIOGRAPHY-001

Status: DESIGN_AND_NEUTRAL_PRESERVATION_RULES_FROZEN_BEFORE_AUTHORITY_TARGET_TASK_PROVIDER_OR_MEASUREMENT

## Purpose

Study 006 tests whether neutral canonical-preservation rules can resolve the exact-value fidelity failures remaining after Study 005 successfully solved schema and graph placement.

Study 005 remains immutable and closed as QUALITY_CENSORED_CANONICAL_VALUE_FIDELITY_FAILURE_AFTER_STRUCTURAL_SUCCESS. Its P1 cost remains rejected-task evidence and is not inherited as an accepted baseline.

## Study Question

When a requesting model receives the already successful value-free schema intervention plus neutral rules requiring exact preservation of authority-supported lexical values, can it reconstruct a quality-equivalent Enriched Biography graph without receiving the completed target, target-to-field mapping, evaluator, Study 005 output, failure locations, or protected P3 payload?

## Experimental Intervention

Study 006 retains the Study 005 schema and graph-placement intervention and adds value-free preservation rules for five failure families:

1. Preserve complete canonical identifiers, including every namespace, prefix, delimiter, path, and fragment.
2. Preserve Unicode trademark and other meaningful marks exactly when present in authority evidence.
3. Use the exact authority-supported ontology token without replacing it with a plausible neighboring type.
4. Copy canonical descriptors, classifications, roles, and claim types without paraphrase or normalization.
5. Reproduce the public retrieval-rights boundary lexically as required by the later frozen contract rather than substituting semantically similar wording.

These are transformation constraints, not target payload values.

## Disclosure Boundary

The neutral preservation rules may expose:

- failure-family names at a generic level;
- exact-preservation requirements;
- prohibited transformation classes;
- generic examples that are not values from the Study 005 target;
- instructions to copy authority-supported values without alteration; and
- the distinction between semantic similarity and exact contractual fidelity.

The neutral preservation rules must not expose:

- any Study 005 target value;
- any field-to-target-value assignment;
- canonical names, identifiers, URLs, versions, prices, networks, assets, roles, classifications, or rights strings from the frozen target;
- expected provenance IDs;
- the Study 005 model output;
- the Study 005 quality evaluation or mismatch locations;
- the completed target representation;
- the evaluator implementation; or
- any protected or paid P3 payload.

## Quality Boundary

The later Study 006 Quality Gate must remain exact and complete. Neutral preservation rules do not relax the target. Broad semantic similarity, normalized spelling, substituted ontology types, omitted marks, shortened identifiers, paraphrased descriptors, or semantically equivalent rights language remain failures when they differ from the frozen contract.

One passing observation may establish initial feasibility but cannot establish stable cost or reliability. Repeated sampling requires a separately frozen calibration plan with accepted-observation targets, stopping rules, retry policy, failure treatment, and interleaving rules.
