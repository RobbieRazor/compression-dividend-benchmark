## Purpose

Study 007 tests whether value-free source-selection and extraction rules can resolve the 14 relationship-target mismatches that remained after Study 006 achieved exact schema placement, subject fidelity, provenance coverage, and retrieval-rights fidelity.

Study 006 remains immutable and closed as quality-censored. Its rejected observation is diagnostic evidence, not an accepted economic baseline.

## Study Question

Can a model select exact relationship-target values from supplied authority evidence when it receives neutral instructions to distinguish canonical field values from page metadata, implementation labels, and explanatory prose, without receiving the protected target, target mappings, prior outputs, failure locations, evaluator, or protected P3 payload?

## Two-Arm Design

### Arm A — Historical-Authority Instruction Isolation

Arm A must reuse the exact Study 006 model-visible authority evidence, neutral schema, provider configuration, and hidden quality target. Its only model-visible intervention relative to Study 006 is the new value-free source-grounding rule set.

Arm A estimates the effect of the instruction intervention under the historical authority boundary.

### Arm B — Refreshed-Authority Architecture Effect

Arm B must use the same neutral task, schema, source-grounding rules, provider configuration, hidden target, and evaluator as Arm A. Its only intended difference is an independently captured post-RRIP 2.1.0 public-authority snapshot, frozen before either arm is measured.

Arm B estimates the incremental effect of refreshed authority architecture relative to Arm A. New source content may change evidentiary availability, but it may not alter or relax the hidden quality target without a separately documented new study.

## Value-Free Intervention

The rules require the model to:

1. Copy explicit authority-supported values exactly rather than translate them into plausible alternatives.
2. Distinguish a governed field value from page type, document type, product label, link label, or descriptive prose.
3. Preserve Unicode marks, controlled tokens, delimiters, casing, and identifier fragments.
4. Resolve competing candidates by field role and explicit authority support, not semantic fluency.
5. Mark an unavailable or ambiguous value unresolved rather than inventing a target-compatible value.

These are selection constraints, not protected payload values or field-to-value mappings.

## Disclosure Boundary

Both arms must hide the completed target, Quality Contract, evaluator, prior Study 005 and 006 outputs and diagnostics, mismatch locations, and protected P3 payload. The refreshed Arm B authority snapshot may contain public facts, but it must be frozen independently of model output and inspected for rights and provenance before use.

## Quality and Sampling Boundary

The exact 9/9 relationship gate and every subject, schema, provenance, and rights criterion remain mandatory. Partial credit and semantic similarity do not change the primary result.

After full preregistration, each arm may receive at most one initial-feasibility observation with no automatic retry. Arm order, observation identifiers, stopping rules, and input hashes must be frozen before the first API call. One passing observation establishes only initial feasibility, not stable cost or reliability.

P2 caching, P3 payment, and economic comparison remain blocked until a quality-equivalent P1 baseline exists and the applicable later calibration package is frozen.

## Cross-Study Boundary

Arm A may be compared descriptively with Study 006 to assess the instruction intervention. Arm B may be compared with Arm A to assess the refreshed-authority effect. Raw costs are not directly interchangeable across changed input boundaries without accounting for input and token differences.

The Pisano Plate is an architectural update but is not evidence for the enriched-biography target and must not be introduced as a task-specific authority source unless a later freeze proves relevance.
