## Purpose

Study 005 tests whether exact machine-schema guidance can resolve the structural fidelity failure observed in Study 004 without revealing protected target payload values.

Study 004 remains immutable and closed as QUALITY_CENSORED_STRUCTURAL_FIDELITY_FAILURE. Its P1 cost is rejected-task evidence only and is not inherited as an accepted baseline.

## Study Question

When the requesting model receives:

1. a value-free neutral output schema;
2. the exact accepted container names and field vocabulary;
3. the nine required relationship-class labels;
4. the relationship-specific target-field placement rules; and
5. the required provenance and retrieval-rights container structure,

can it reconstruct a quality-equivalent Enriched Biography relationship graph using only separately frozen public authority evidence, while the target values, target representation, evaluator, protected P3 payload, and all Study 004 model outputs remain hidden?
